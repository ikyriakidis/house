What changed from Plot 2 v5
  • The same house on Plot 1, 13 m wide instead of 15.3 m so it fits between the 3 m side setbacks. The bedroom wing keeps its sizes; the 2.3 m comes out of the east wing and the courtyard.
  • The garage turns to open south onto the street, with an 8 m driveway beside the front path. Both the front door and the garage are reached from the one street.
  • The living room is 7 m wide instead of 9.3 m, still with glass along the whole courtyard side and the sofa centred on the pool. The dining room (3.3 m) and kitchen (3.7 m) behind it are narrower; the table turns north–south and the island is 1.8 m.
  • No separate tech room: the batteries, inverter, panel and rack go on a tech wall inside the garage, which is 7 m wide to make room for it.
  • Pool 3 × 8 m. The courtyard runs to the east boundary wall, so the terrace, deck and loungers use the 3 m side setback.
  • Heated area about 182 m² (Plot 2 v5: 208 m²); the tech room moved into the garage accounts for 6.6 m² of that.
